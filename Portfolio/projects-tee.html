Highschool Class Tee Design

SM3 SCI

This is my high school class t-shirt that I designed, which won 1st place. It uses a comic style, and all the drawings are hand-drawn by me. Each frame is unique and represents one person along with their “dark history.” I created it with the intention of preserving the fun times I had in high school and all the jokes we shared. And of course, I couldn't forget to include my homeroom teacher right in the middle.

"Not always a scientist."

Design Details:

  • My Class "Dark History"
  • Color Palette Matching
